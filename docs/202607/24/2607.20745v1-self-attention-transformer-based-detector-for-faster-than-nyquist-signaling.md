## 摘要
本研究提出了一种新颖的仅编码器Transformer接收机架构，用于通过超奈奎斯特（FTN）信令信道传输的BPSK信号，该信道在压缩因子τ=0.8下引入了有意的符号间干扰（ISI）。构建并评估了一个完整的端到端通信链路，包括BPSK调制、RRC脉冲成形以及匹配滤波产生的ISI系数。所提出的Transformer接收机在0-8 dB的E_b/N_0范围内与最优BCJR检测器进行了基准对比。为了系统地缩小与BCJR的误码率差距，开发了一种结合多信噪比预训练和逐信噪比课程微调的两阶段训练策略。与基于GRU的接收机相比，分析了Transformer接收机的计算复杂度和推理延迟。注意力图可视化显示，Transformer能够自主识别FTN引起的ISI记忆结构，无需任何先验信道知识；随着信噪比的增加，注意力权重显著集中在中心令牌及其最近邻上。

## Abstract
In this study, a novel encoder-only Transformer-based receiver architecture is presented for BPSK signals transmitted over Faster-than-Nyquist (FTN) signaling channels that introduce intentional inter-symbol interference (ISI) with a compression factor of $τ=0.8$. A complete end-to-end communication chain encompassing BPSK modulation, RRC pulse shaping, and the ISI coefficients arising from matched filtering was constructed and evaluated. The proposed Transformer receiver was benchmarked against the optimal BCJR detector over an $E_b/N_0$ range of 0-8 dB. To systematically close the BER gap to the BCJR, a two-stage training strategy combining multi-SNR pretraining and per-SNR curriculum fine-tuning was developed. The computational complexity and inference latency of the Transformer receiver were analyzed in comparison with a GRU based receiver. Attention map visualizations revealed that the Transformer autonomously identifies the FTN-induced ISI memory structure without requiring any prior channel knowledge; as the SNR increases, the attention weights become significantly concentrated around the center token and its nearest neighbors.
